ABOUT ME

I always thought Eren was a rare name, but on my first day of high school there were three Erens in my class.
To tell us apart, my classmates started calling me "Eren the Koç".

The name stuck, so it became the name of this website.

Where I come from

I'm from İstanbul. I studied medicine in Turkey, spent an Erasmus semester in Berlin, and have worked for over ten years in hospitals (in Turkey and Germany) and a public health office in Berlin.

Where I'm going

Computers and music have always been part of my life: an IT competition and a Flash course as a teenager, video editing and music for a local TV station, and years of making music in DAWs, both with my band Hi, Balloon and in my solo project Yedi Kat Yabancı.

What I build

I'm a beginner, and I learn by building things I care about.
My DIY bitcrusher guitar pedal (Arduino, C++) became my first JUCE plugin, which runs as a VST3 in Ableton Live. I also built a portfolio website for an artist and two small browser games in vanilla JavaScript.

Right now I'm focusing on JavaScript and C/C++. See the projects.

What I'm looking for

From May 2027 I'm looking for a mandatory internship in Berlin (6 to 8 months): audio and plugin development, programming music hardware, or games.

Languages

Turkish (native), German (C1), English (fluent), Spanish (basic).
